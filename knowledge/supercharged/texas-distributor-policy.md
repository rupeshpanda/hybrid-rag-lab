## §1 Applicability

This regional policy applies to distributors operating in Texas, currently Lone Star Auto Supply and
Dallas Power Distributors. It supplements, and does not replace, the applicable tier or contract return
policy.

## §2 Restocking Fee Disclosure

Texas-based distributors must be given the restocking fee percentage in writing at the time a return is
authorized, before the unit is shipped back. This is a disclosure requirement, not a change to the fee
amount itself, which is still set by tier or contract.

## §3 Regional Freight Note

Returns from Texas distributors to the Supercharged distribution center use a regional freight carrier
with a shorter transit time than other regions. This does not change the return window calculation, which
is based on the request date relative to the invoice date, not the physical transit date.

## §4 Precedence

Where this regional policy and a distributor-tier or contract policy would produce different outcomes on
eligibility, dollar amount, or approval, the distributor-tier or contract policy governs. This policy
governs disclosure and process only.
