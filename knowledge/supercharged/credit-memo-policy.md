## §1 Purpose

A credit memo is the financial document that settles an approved return or warranty claim against the
original invoice. This policy describes how credit memos relate to invoices and returns.

## §2 One Credit Memo per Approved Refund

Each approved refund or warranty credit is settled by exactly one credit memo referencing the specific
return and the specific invoice. A single invoice may accumulate multiple credit memos over time if it has
been the subject of more than one partial return.

## §3 Checking Refund History

Before evaluating a new return against an invoice, check for existing credit memos against that invoice.
An existing credit memo does not automatically block a new return, but the previously refunded quantity
must be subtracted from the invoiced quantity when checking whether a new request would exceed what is
still available to return.

## §4 Full Settlement

An invoice is considered fully refunded when the sum of quantities covered by its credit memos equals the
invoiced quantity, or when a credit memo explicitly covers the full invoiced amount. No further refund can
be issued against a fully refunded invoice.

## §5 Audit Trail

Every credit memo should be traceable to its return and its invoice, so that a distributor question such as
"has this invoice already received a credit" can be answered directly from structured data rather than from
memory or estimation.
