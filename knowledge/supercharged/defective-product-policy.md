## §1 Scope

This policy governs how Supercharged Battery Co. triages any battery reported as defective, whether
unopened or already installed, across all product categories: AGM, Standard, Heavy Duty, Marine, and EV
Auxiliary.

## §2 Unopened, Reported Defective

An unopened unit reported defective before installation is still processed as a normal return under the
distributor's applicable tier or contract return policy, provided it is within the return window. It is
not treated as a warranty claim, since it has not yet entered service.

## §3 Installed, Reported Defective

An installed unit reported defective is never eligible for a normal return. It must be evaluated against
the product's specific warranty policy (for example, the AGM Battery Warranty Policy for AGM-series
batteries). If no product-specific warranty document exists for the category, the general warranty terms
in this policy apply: 12 months from invoice date for Standard and Marine batteries, 18 months for Heavy
Duty batteries, and 36 months for EV Auxiliary batteries, unless a product-specific policy states
otherwise.

## §4 Reported Failure Modes Covered

Covered failure modes include: will not hold a charge, will not start the vehicle or vessel at rated cold
cranking amps, internal cell failure, and case swelling not caused by external heat exposure. Failure
modes outside this list should be escalated to a human reviewer rather than resolved automatically.

## §5 Warranty Outcome

A warranty claim that is within coverage and matches a covered failure mode is approved for replacement or
credit at no cost to the distributor, with no restocking fee. A claim outside the coverage window is
declined and the distributor is informed the unit is out of warranty.

## §6 Relationship to Refund Rules

Warranty claims are tracked separately from the normal-return refund amount in this lab's evidence panel,
because they represent a replacement or warranty credit rather than a return-driven refund. Manager
approval thresholds in the Manager Approval Policy apply to the dollar value of normal-return refunds; they
do not gate straightforward in-warranty claims within the coverage window.
