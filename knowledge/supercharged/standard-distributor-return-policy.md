## §1 Applicability

This policy applies to distributors holding Standard tier status, the entry tier for new Supercharged
Battery Co. distributors. Where a Standard-tier distributor's individual contract sets different terms,
for example an extended return window negotiated at signing, the contract governs over this policy.

## §2 Return Window

Unopened products purchased by Standard-tier distributors may be returned within 30 days of the invoice
date. This is the shortest return window of the three tiers.

## §3 Restocking Fee

A 15% restocking fee applies to normal returns of unopened product from Standard-tier distributors. Refund
equals unit price × quantity returned × 85%.

## §4 Installed or Defective Product

Installed product is never eligible for a normal return. Reported-defective installed units are evaluated
under the Defective Product Policy and the applicable product warranty policy, independent of tier.

## §5 Approval Threshold

Standard-tier distributors have the lowest automatic refund approval threshold of the three tiers. See the
Manager Approval Policy for the current value.

## §6 Growing Into Gold or Silver

Distributors are periodically reviewed for tier upgrades based on purchase volume and payment history.
Tier changes are applied prospectively and do not retroactively change the terms of returns already
requested or invoices already issued.
