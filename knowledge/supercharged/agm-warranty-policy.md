## §1 Coverage

Supercharged AGM-series batteries (AGM-100, AGM-105, and future AGM SKUs) carry a 24-month warranty from
the original invoice date against manufacturing defects, including failure to hold a charge, failure to
start under rated cold cranking amps, and internal cell failure. This applies regardless of distributor
tier and covers units that have already been installed.

## §2 Installed, In-Warranty, Reported Defective

An AGM unit that has been installed and is reported defective within 24 months of the invoice date
qualifies for warranty evaluation. It is not processed as a normal return and is not subject to any
restocking fee. Warranty units are inspected or, for the purposes of this lab, treated as approved for
warranty replacement or credit once the reported issue matches a covered failure mode and the unit is
within the coverage window.

## §3 Exclusions

The warranty does not cover damage from physical abuse, incorrect installation, incorrect application
(for example, using a marine battery specification in place of an AGM), water damage, or damage from a
charging system fault outside the battery itself. This lab does not model root-cause inspection findings;
warranty eligibility here is based on coverage window and reported failure mode matching a covered
category.

## §4 Out-of-Warranty Units

An AGM unit reported defective more than 24 months after the invoice date is out of warranty. It does not
qualify for warranty evaluation and does not qualify as a normal return once installed. The distributor
should be informed the unit is out of coverage.

## §5 Relationship to Distributor Tier

Warranty coverage terms are the same across Gold, Silver, and Standard tiers. Tier only affects the return
window, restocking fee, and approval threshold for normal returns of unopened product, not warranty
coverage on installed product.

## §6 Interaction With Mixed Returns

When a single return request includes both unopened units and installed, reported-defective AGM units, the
unopened units are evaluated under the applicable distributor return policy and the installed units are
evaluated under this warranty policy, independently.
