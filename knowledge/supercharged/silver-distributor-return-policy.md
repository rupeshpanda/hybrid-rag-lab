## §1 Applicability

This policy applies to distributors holding Silver tier status. Where a Silver distributor's individual
contract sets different terms, the contract governs.

## §2 Return Window

Unopened products purchased by Silver-tier distributors may be returned within 60 days of the invoice
date, twice the Standard-tier window.

## §3 Restocking Fee

A 10% restocking fee applies to normal returns of unopened product from Silver-tier distributors. Refund
equals unit price × quantity returned × 90%.

## §4 Installed or Defective Product

Installed product is not eligible for a normal return regardless of tier. Reported-defective installed
units are evaluated under the Defective Product Policy and the applicable product warranty policy.

## §5 Approval Threshold

Silver-tier distributors have a mid-level automatic refund approval threshold, lower than Gold and higher
than Standard. See the Manager Approval Policy for the current value. A refund request that would exceed
this threshold requires manager approval before a credit memo is issued, even if every other eligibility
rule is satisfied.

## §6 Quantity and Duplicate Refund Rules

Company-wide rules on quantity limits and duplicate refunds apply without modification to Silver-tier
distributors.
