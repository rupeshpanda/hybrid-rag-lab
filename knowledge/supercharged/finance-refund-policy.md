## §1 Purpose

This policy governs how the Finance department processes refunds once a return has been determined
eligible under the applicable distributor return policy and, where relevant, a warranty policy.

## §2 Refund Calculation

For a normal return: refund amount = unit price × quantity of eligible unopened units × (1 − restocking
fee percentage for the distributor's tier or contract). Restocking fee percentage is 0% for Gold, 10% for
Silver, and 15% for Standard, unless a contract states otherwise.

## §3 Existing Refund Check

Before issuing a new credit memo, Finance must confirm the invoice has not already been fully refunded.
An invoice with an existing credit memo covering the full invoiced amount cannot receive an additional
refund. A partial prior credit memo does not block a further refund for the remaining eligible quantity,
but the combined refunded quantity across all credit memos still cannot exceed the invoiced quantity.

## §4 Credit Memo Issuance

Once a refund is approved (automatically, or after manager approval where required), Finance issues a
credit memo referencing the return and the original invoice. The credit memo amount should equal the
approved refund amount.

## §5 Warranty Claims

Warranty claims approved under a product warranty policy or the Defective Product Policy are processed as
warranty credits or replacements, tracked separately from normal-return refunds, and follow the same
credit memo issuance process once approved.

## §6 Approval Gate

Any refund whose amount exceeds the distributor's applicable approval threshold must be routed for manager
approval before Finance issues a credit memo. See the Manager Approval Policy for threshold values and how
they are determined.
