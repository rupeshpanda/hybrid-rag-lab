## §1 Applicability

This policy applies to distributors holding Gold tier status, Supercharged Battery Co.'s highest volume
tier. Where a Gold distributor's individual contract sets different terms, the contract governs.

## §2 Return Window

Unopened products purchased by Gold distributors may be returned within 90 days of the invoice date. This
is three times the Standard-tier window, reflecting the volume and tenure of Gold-tier relationships.

## §3 Restocking Fee

Gold-tier distributors are not charged a restocking fee on normal returns of unopened product. The refund
for a qualifying normal return equals the full invoiced unit price multiplied by the quantity returned.

## §4 Installed or Defective Product

As with all tiers, installed product is not eligible for a normal return. Installed units reported
defective must be routed to the Defective Product Policy and the relevant product warranty policy. Gold
tier does not change warranty coverage, which is governed separately by product warranty policies.

## §5 Approval Threshold

Gold-tier distributors have a higher automatic refund approval threshold than Silver or Standard tiers,
reflecting their transaction volume. See the Manager Approval Policy for the current threshold value.
Refunds at or below the threshold do not require manager approval; refunds above it do.

## §6 Quantity and Duplicate Refund Rules

The company-wide rules on quantity limits, duplicate refunds, and refund calculation in the Standard
Company Return Policy apply in full to Gold-tier distributors. Gold status changes the window, the
restocking fee, and the approval threshold. It does not relax quantity or duplicate-refund controls.

## §7 Mixed Returns

A single return request from a Gold distributor may combine unopened units eligible for a normal return
with installed, reported-defective units that require warranty evaluation. Each unit group is evaluated
independently under its applicable policy, and the response should state both outcomes clearly.
