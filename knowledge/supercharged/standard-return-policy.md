## §1 Purpose

This is the baseline return policy for Supercharged Battery Co. and applies to every distributor unless a
distributor-tier policy or an individual distributor contract states otherwise. Tier policies and contracts
are more specific and take precedence over this document wherever they conflict.

## §2 Eligibility

A battery is eligible for a normal return when it is unopened, in its original packaging, and returned
within the applicable return window measured from the invoice date. The default return window under this
baseline policy is 30 days. Distributor tiers and contracts may extend this window.

## §3 Restocking Fee

A restocking fee applies to normal returns of unopened product. The default restocking fee is 15% of the
invoiced unit price for the returned quantity. Distributor tiers may reduce or waive this fee.

## §4 Installed or Used Product

Product that has been installed in a vehicle or vessel is not eligible for a normal return under this
policy. Installed product that is reported defective must be evaluated under the Defective Product Policy
and, where applicable, the relevant product warranty policy instead.

## §5 Quantity Limits

The quantity returned against an invoice can never exceed the quantity originally invoiced. Requests that
exceed the invoiced quantity must be rejected and referred back to the distributor for correction.

## §6 Refund Calculation

Refund amount equals (unit price × quantity returned) minus the applicable restocking fee, for units that
qualify as a normal return. Defective or warranty units are handled separately and are not subject to the
restocking fee.

## §7 Duplicate Refunds

An invoice that has already been fully refunded through a credit memo cannot be refunded again. Finance
must check for an existing credit memo against the invoice before approving any new refund.

## §8 Approval

Refunds above a distributor's approval threshold require manager approval before a credit memo is issued.
See the Manager Approval Policy for threshold values by tier and any contract-level overrides.
